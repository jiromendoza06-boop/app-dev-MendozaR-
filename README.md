## CARS

### Plot
The film follows rookie racer car, *Lightning McQueen*, and his pursuit of the prestigious *Piston Cup*. Confident in his abilities, he views himself as a one-man team and often distances himself from others whom he considers less capable. After a historic race ends in a three-way tie, a tiebreaker race is scheduled to determine the true champion. While traveling to the event, McQueen becomes separated from his convoy and accidentally damages the road in the small town of *Radiator Springs*. Forced to stay and repair the damage, he meets the town's residents and gradually forms meaningful friendships with them. Among them is the legendary *Hudson Hornet*, a former champion racer who becomes McQueen's mentor and teaches him valuable lessons about humility, friendships, and what truly matters in life.
